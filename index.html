Fundamentals of Management — Interactive Worksheets

One interactive worksheet for each of the 32 lectures. Use them in class to demonstrate a concept, or assign them as practice. Everything runs in the browser — no login, no install, works on phones.

16 weeks32 worksheets160 activitiesCLO1–CLO5Text: Robbins & Coulter, Management

Every worksheet has five tabs

1 · What-if calculatorSliders that apply a fixed formula (e.g. break-even, span of control, expectancy). Change the inputs and watch the result.
2 · Sorting exerciseClick items into the right category, or put steps in order, then check your answers.
3 · Dilemma scenarioMake two linked decisions in a Pakistani workplace case and see how they move three scores.
4 · SelectorWeight the criteria that matter and see which option — strategy, structure, style or tool — fits best.
5 · Concept checkFive multiple-choice questions with instant feedback.

Figures in these worksheets are simplified or illustrative for teaching; verify real-world data before using it in assignments.
